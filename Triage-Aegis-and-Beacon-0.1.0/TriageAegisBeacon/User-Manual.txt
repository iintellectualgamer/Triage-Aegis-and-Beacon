Install or update
1. Close World of Warcraft. Extract Triage-Aegis-and-Beacon-0.1.0.zip.
2. Copy the inner TriageAegisBeacon folder into your edition's Interface\AddOns folder.
3. Confirm TriageAegisBeacon.toc is directly inside that folder.
4. Launch the same edition. Enable Triage - Aegis & Beacon from AddOns, then log in.
5. Type /triage version. This release shows 0.1.0 / Build 22.

For updates, overwrite the files in the existing TriageAegisBeacon folder with WoW closed.
Your character settings are stored in WTF.
Read the bundled User-Manual.txt or download the standalone PDF edition of this guide.
The addon's spell and talent lists follow the game client and current character build as
you move between zones, instances, and earlier expansion content within that client.

Open Triage
Use /triage show or left-click the gold beacon on the minimap. Right-click the beacon to open
Settings.

Healing loadout and emergency healing
Open All heals on the healing panel or type /triage heals. It shows the learned healing
spells for your current specialization and build with their health-bar gestures and keys.
It follows spellbook, rank, talent, and specialization changes. Show all clicks lists every
current gesture. Search by spell name, ID, or gesture and select a row to edit the click.
Keys that are already in use take priority. If WoW is protecting bindings in
combat, the addon applies changes when combat ends.

The Emergency button shows your selected emergency spell and its cooldown where the
client supports it. Press Alt + right-click on a health bar or hover an ally and press
the keyboard shortcut. Alt + E is preferred when free. A red underline marks living
units below 30% health.

Emergency Auto target starts on. Outside combat, pressing Emergency with no living
friendly mouseover checks for the wounded ally with the lowest readable health percentage
in confirmed range. If there is no eligible choice, Emergency can use your friendly
target. In combat, use the ally's health bar, friendly mouseover, or selected target.
With Target player on healing clicks enabled in Mouse buttons, supported healing actions
also select the recipient. WoW still applies the spell's normal cast requirements.

In Healing loadout, turn off Auto clicks, Auto keys, and Auto target for manual setup.
Saved clicks and manual WoW bindings remain available. To choose an emergency spell,
edit Alt + right-click in Custom spells. The selected spell's usual conditions apply.
In the same panel, Quiet Group pauses automatic range chat, whispers, pings, and
group-start buff notices. Manual markers and alerts remain available.

2. Panel and appearance
Keep combat tools close and move deeper setup to the settings window.

Control / Action
  Party  -  Open your health bar and up to four party bars. In a raid, this is your own
  subgroup. Appearance can add a pet bar.
  Raid  -  See up to 40 clickable players in WoW's eight numbered subgroups.
  Markers  -  Open world flags, party and target icons, ready check, and pull countdown.
  Combined  -  Switch automatically between the party and full raid above markers.
  Settings  -  Open Custom spells, Healing loadout, Spell icons, Mouse buttons, Practice, Range alerts,
  Buff coverage, Appearance, Keybinds, Profiles, or Credits.
  Minus / plus  -  Collapse to the title bar or restore the current view.
  X  -  Hide the play panel. Use the minimap button or /triage show to bring it back.
  Title bar  -  Drag to place the play panel outside combat.

Minimap beacon
Left-click to show or hide the play panel. Right-click for Settings. Drag to move it around
the minimap edge. Use /triage minimap reset to restore its position and visibility.

Appearance controls
Set panel opacity from 25% to 100%, scale to 85%, 100%, or 115%, and choose Apply appearance.
Choose Compact or Comfortable party spacing, panel colors, and the pet or minimap button.
Show party roles displays Tank, Healer, DPS, or Unassigned. Settings keeps its normal opacity.

Health readouts
Show current health and Show health percentages are separate switches in Appearance.
Apply appearance to show both, either one, or just the health fill. They apply to Party,
Raid, and the optional pet bar. Large health values use the game's abbreviated format.
Raid and Combined in a raid use a wider grid for both readouts and the ! control.
When you show a single readout, it can use space freed by the hidden readout.
When a readable unit state is available, Offline, Dead, or Ghost replaces the numbers.

Shield, healing, and mana cues
Show absorbs and incoming heals in Appearance controls the thin strips at the top of
health bars. Blue marks damage absorption, amber marks healing absorption, and green
marks incoming healing reported by your client. Each strip fills independently and
is scaled to the unit's maximum health. The game supplies these values.

On supported Midnight and WoW Forever clients, enable Show a Private Aura icon in
Appearance to add one game-managed Private Aura slot per player bar. Apply appearance
outside combat to change the setting. Use WoW's boss warnings for encounter alerts.

Classic and WoW Forever can show your mana and threat below the healing bars. High
threat means you are competing for threat. Aggro on you means a creature is targeting
you according to WoW's threat data. On visible group bars, amber edges show high
threat and red edges show aggro where WoW exposes the state. On WoW Forever and
older Classic clients with the five-second rule, the 5s swipe marks the approximate
wait after observed spell mana spending. Use Show mana and threat cues in Appearance.

Open Practice and select Sample cues to preview shields, healing absorption, incoming
healing, and critical health. The sample bar still lets you test your gestures.

Priority players and simple cues
Pin the tank below Self in Party or pin characters within their own raid subgroups.
Regrouping and pins update after combat. A health-bar tooltip shows the player's name
when available and the active assignments for Left-click, Middle-click, and Right-click.
Hold Ctrl, Alt, or Shift over the bar to see the active clicks for those modifiers.
Combat tooltips are optional and start off. Use Custom spells > Saved clicks for every
binding. The bar gets a thin gold outline while hovered. Find Show health-bar hover
outline in Appearance, then use Apply appearance to change it.


Combat controls
Use the layout tabs, title controls, minimap button, automatic alert switch, or marker shortcut
during combat. Settings stays available. Changes to spell and mouse assignments, the healer
shortcut, panel size, and priority order take effect after combat. Opacity, bar colors, and
health labels can update immediately. Move the panel and assign game keys outside combat.

3. Healing spells and keys
Choose your spells once and put the casts you use most within reach.


Assign mouse clicks
Open Settings > Custom spells, or choose Assign spells from Party, Raid, or Combined.
Left-click, Middle-click, and Right-click start with all modifiers off. Toggle Alt, Ctrl, and Shift.
Use the arrows by Buttons 1-5 to reach Button 31. These filters cover all 248 combinations
the addon can assign when WoW receives the mouse input.

Select Pick, type a learned spell's name or ID, or drag its icon from the WoW spellbook or
action bar onto the field or Pick. Triage copies the spell ID and leaves the icon on your
cursor. Return an action-bar icon to its slot, or cancel a spellbook drag. A typed or copied
ID stays visible. Retail uses the name when it maps to the same spell. Forever and Classic
clients with ranked spells preserve the learned rank for an exact spell ID.
Edits stay in the form as you switch modifiers or button groups.
Choose Save spells to apply them outside combat. Look for "Assigned spells saved." in
Settings and your chat window. If you save in combat, Triage confirms again when the
new bindings are active. Hover a bar to preview what your click will do, and hold Ctrl
to check the Ctrl + left-click assignment.
A blank field returns that click to its empty-field behavior. Blank Left-click and
Alt + middle-click select the unit. Other blank clicks have no action.

Auto-fill single and Auto-fill area restore the suggested spells for their original tile
gestures listed below. Changes saved during combat apply after combat.

Review saved custom spells
In Custom spells, open Saved clicks to see stored assignments for your current profile.
Each row shows its full mouse gesture and the assignment, with a name and ID when available.
Check whether the assignment is active, unavailable in the current build, disabled, or set
to apply after combat. Select a row to jump to its field. Save spells applies any edits.

Search spells and talents
Open Pick > Browse spells and talents beside an assignment field. Search by name or spell
ID. Click All abilities to cycle through Assignable and Talents. Click an Assignable row to
fill the field, then save on the original settings page. Hover a row to see its spell ID
and details. On clients with ranked spells, the browser lists learned ranks separately.

The same browser is available under Custom spells, Mouse buttons, and extra Spell icons.
Triage reads learned healing, damage, tank, utility, racial, and pet spells from the
client. It includes learned abilities inside spellbook groups and shows class, hero, PvP,
Forever Legacy, and Classic talents where the game exposes them. Open the browser on the class
and specialization you want to set up. Passive spells, unlearned talents, choices with no
cast, and replaced spells have a status label. Assignable entries can be saved to a click.

Talent changes and saved assignments
Apply talent and hero changes in WoW. The picker refreshes with the new build. A stored
spell becomes available again when you relearn it. It follows the active replacement
when the game reports one for that spell ID. Cooldowns, forms, range, and PvP rules still
apply when you cast.

Choose zero through ten tiles
Open Settings > Spell icons or type /triage icons. The count selects the first slots in
order. Use the checkboxes to choose individual tiles. Pick spells for the two extras,
enter a name or ID, or drop a learned spell onto the field or Pick. Saved fields show the
name and ID together. A blank field leaves that extra slot empty.
Select Save spell icons. The choices apply to Party, Raid, and Combined. Choosing zero
collapses the spell rows so the health bars move up. Your health-bar gestures and spell
keys still work. Icon changes saved during combat apply when combat ends.

Unit bar click              Action
Left-click                  Select this unit by default
Middle-click                 Learned friendly dispel by default, when available
Right-click                  Single target 2
Shift + left-click           Single target 1
Ctrl + left-click            Single target 3
Shift + right-click          Area healing 1
Alt + left-click             Area healing 2
Ctrl + right-click           Area healing 3
Alt + right-click            Emergency spell
Alt + middle-click           Select this unit by default

Configure plain mouse buttons
Use Settings > Mouse buttons or /triage mouse for Left-click, Right-click, Middle-click,
and the Alt + right-click emergency spell. All click combinations opens Assign spells.
Both pages edit the same assignments. Triage suggests a learned emergency spell for healer
classes. Restore mouse defaults restores left, middle, and emergency and enables targeting
on healing clicks.

Plain and modifier tiles
Plain Left-click, Middle-click, and Right-click have separate tiles. Shift and Ctrl
assignments appear in the Modifiers rows. All three healing views use the same assignments.
Target (/target) shows which health-bar gesture selects the clicked player. None hides its
tile. Hidden or unavailable tiles leave the space available to the rest of the row.

Each spell tile shows its name, ID, gesture, and any Triage keyboard shortcut. Hover to see
the full details. Use left, right, or middle-click on a spell tile for a quick cast.

Holy Paladin and utility ID examples
  212056  Absolution
  125439  Revive Battle Pets
  6940    Blessing of Sacrifice
  200025  Beacon of Virtue
  53563   Beacon of Light
The game supplies localized names and determines availability. If a talent replaces a
saved spell, the field shows the saved spell while the tile shows the active cast.

Practice the live assignments
Open Settings > Practice or type /triage practice. For a quick setup, assign one or two
heals, then try those clicks on the practice target. It displays the gesture and active
action without casting. Press a side button here to see which number WoW receives.

Select a player directly
Type /target or Target into any health-bar spell field, or choose Target player under Pick.
Save, then use that click on a party or raid bar to set WoW's current target. For example,
leave Holy Shock on Left-click and assign /target to Ctrl + left-click. Enter None or choose
No action under Pick to disable a click, even if its blank default selects a player.

Target the player you heal
Target player on healing clicks starts on for each character. When you click a party or
raid health bar with a supported friendly spell, Triage casts at that unit and selects it.
Heal a different teammate to switch your target. This works on plain and modified clicks.
Selection can still happen if WoW rejects the cast for cooldown or range. To keep your
current target while healing, clear the checkbox in Settings > Mouse buttons. The checkbox
saves immediately. Spell tiles and keyboard shortcuts retain their normal targeting.
Assignment changes saved during combat apply after combat.

Solo Flash of Light test
Open Assign spells with all modifiers off. Put Flash of Light in Right-click and save.
Right-click the Self bar.
You can also left- or right-click its tile. Check the cast bar or mana while standing still
with the spell ready.

Quick tiles, keys, and Beacons
Use the health bar gestures to aim unit-targeted heals at the clicked character. Quick tiles
and spell keys try a living friendly mouseover, then a living friendly target. Standard heals
can fall back to Self. Ground, hostile, self, pet, and revive actions follow their own
targeting. Beacon of Light and Beacon of Faith require a friendly mouseover or target on
tiles and keys. Test Beacon of Faith on a teammate with an available Beacon slot. Settings >
Keybinds > Assign in WoW shows both sets of spell keys.

Transfer profiles
Settings > Profiles selects an export string for Ctrl+C. Paste it on another character and
choose Import profile. TAB6 includes all click bindings, spells, and icon choices for the
active specialization or talent group. Triage validates every slot before applying
it. Older TAB1 through TAB5 exports apply the fields they contain. Target and None click
assignments travel with the profile. The Target player on healing clicks setting is saved
for each character.

Dungeon and Raid presets
In Profiles, use Save Dungeon or Save Raid to store your spells, all click assignments,
and icon choices for this specialization. Use Dungeon opens Party and loads its saved setup.
Use Raid opens Raid and loads its saved setup. Switch presets outside combat.

For Holy Paladins, Divine Toll casts Holy Shock for healing when aimed at an ally. In

Cleanse cue
Turn on Show a cleanse cue in Appearance for a thin blue mark on the edge of a health

5. Range alerts and markers
Coordinate positioning with group markers, alerts, and built-in WoW actions.


Automatic range alerts
Auto alerts starts off in fresh settings. Use the panel button to toggle automatic reminders.
In Alert settings > Channels, choose the automatic channel and check Enable automatic alerts
and Send chat announcements. Save alerts applies the choices. Under Behavior, Send native
minimap ping is a separate switch and starts off. When WoW blocks a native ping, Triage can
show its local minimap mark.

Choose the automatic chat channel
Open Alert settings > Channels. The left column shows the automatic destinations. The
right column shows the manual destinations. The selected button is highlighted in each
column. Choose Current group, Raid (/raid), Party (/p), Instance (/i), Whisper (/w), Say (/s),
or Battleground (/i). Current group chooses /party, /raid, or /instance for your group.
Battleground uses /i for battleground teammates. Automatic Say prepares a /say draft for
you to send. Automatic Whisper addresses confirmed out-of-range group members individually.
An unavailable channel pauses chat until it is available. If an older build saved automatic
Whisper, Test 13 and later switch it to Current group and turn Auto alerts off once on update.

Automatic checks require readable, confirmed range. When a spell check is unavailable,
Triage may show a fallback to WoW's group range. A large raid is checked in batches of
up to ten units per timer tick. Turning automatic alerts off stops its timer. Triage waits
at least 30 seconds between native minimap requests and between group chat requests.
Automatic range and buff announcements share one 30-second group chat delay. WoW's
local range cues can update when the healing bars are visible and reminders are paused.
Use Quiet Group in All heals to pause automatic outgoing reminders together.
Under Behavior, Repeat while out of range allows another notice after the saved delay.

Manual alerts and audio
The manual button starts as Manual mark and shows a quiet marker on your own minimap.
In Alert settings > Channels, check Announce on manual click. Choose a channel in the
Manual chat channel column, edit the Announcement if desired, and select Save alerts.
For a manual ping as well, open Behavior and check Add native minimap ping.
The default message reads "Please come closer to the healer".
Outgoing reminders start with [Triage - Aegis & Beacon] before the player or message.

Action                       Effect
Party Notify or Raid !, left-click  -  Use your manual settings for the selected group member.
Right-click or middle-click  -  Open the channel menu, including Whisper this player.
Mark healer                  Mark yourself with a green triangle.
Ping target                  Request WoW's Assist ping on a selected friendly group target.
Out of range / Far           Show readable or client-displayed range status on a health bar.

Raid teammate alerts
Use ! at the upper-right of a teammate's raid bar. The symbol brightens gently while you
hover and returns to normal when the pointer leaves. The rest of the box remains available
for healing clicks. Turn on Announce on manual click for left-click chat on these buttons.

Manual chat destinations
The visible channel buttons include Whisper, Raid, Say, and Battleground. More channels
offers Yell, Guild, Officer, Raid warning, Emote, and Joined channel. For a joined channel,
enter its name or number, such as /2. For a whisper from the main manual button, enter
Name-Realm or leave it blank to use your friendly target. Notify and ! address that teammate.
If WoW blocks a manual chat request, copy the draft shown under Range alerts and paste it
into WoW chat. The addon leaves your chat input in place.

Ping audio and shared groups
In Behavior, Mute game ping sounds changes the WoW ping audio setting for your client. G still opens
WoW's ping controls. Triage pauses native minimap pings while you type or aim a spell.
Multiple players can use Triage in the same group. When readable events are available,
Triage briefly holds matching automatic group chat and waits after a teammate's minimap ping.
Private whispers are separate. You can choose one player to enable automatic reminders for
the quietest group test.

World markers and unit icons
In Markers or Combined, left-click a world marker and click the ground. Right-click clears
that world marker. Clear all removes world markers. For a unit icon, choose Target, Self, or
Party 1-4 and click an icon. Clear icon removes the selected unit's mark.

The marker symbols are about 15% smaller inside Triage, and the section saves 24 UI pixels
of height. In Settings > Keybinds, bind Marker list to open the markers temporarily from
Party or Raid.

Ready check and pull
Ready check uses WoW's group prompt. 10s pull uses the client countdown when that feature is
available. WoW validates your group permissions. Direct keys can place or clear markers. The
marker-list hotkey can open its view during combat.

Check shareable group buffs and speak only from verified results.

Tracked buff / Provider class

What a scan checks
Use Settings > Buff coverage > Scan now outside combat. The scan includes every raid slot
and checks visible, living, connected players for each tracked buff with a provider. Your
own provider spells are checked against your spellbook. Teammate coverage is based on class
and availability. Unreadable auras and unavailable players are counted as unchecked.

Group-start announcements
In Settings > Buff coverage, check Announce missing buffs when a group or instance starts
and choose Save preferences. This starts off separately from Range alerts. Triage sends one
[Triage - Aegis & Beacon] summary per group or instance. Raid Finder and Timewalking use
instance chat. Combat defers the check. Triage retries incomplete scans and reports verified
counts and unchecked players. A summary from another copy can cancel a duplicate.

Choose a manual destination
Draft announcement writes a message in Settings. Choose Current group, Party, Raid, Instance /
battleground, Raid warning, Say, Yell, Guild, Officer, Emote, Whisper, or Numbered channel.
Enter the player name or channel number when requested. Review and copy the draft, then paste
it into WoW chat when ready to send.

Turn off notices
Uncheck the automatic buff option and choose Save preferences. To omit an individual buff from
scans, uncheck that buff and save.

8. Addon compatibility
Keep the same healing setup when you use Triage alongside other addons.

NoHatersBullyElitists 1.0.1 compatibility update
The separate NoHatersBullyElitists update matches "loser" and "losers" as whole words.
It accepts Triage's default "Please come closer to the healer" in group chat and whispers.
Older flags and stored chat remain in that addon's history. Its warnings and blacklist
are local to the client. Reporting requires a player action.

If you still use NoHatersBullyElitists 1.0.0
Version 1.0.0 can flag Triage's default reminder when it finds "loser" inside "closer".
Open Triage's Alert settings, enter "Please move within healing range" in Announcement,
and click Save alerts. To restore the original reminder after updating NoHatersBullyElitists,
clear the Announcement field and save.

Retail chat and large logs
The separate NoHatersBullyElitists update declares Retail 12.1.5, skips chat values WoW
withholds from addons, and refreshes the small counters without rebuilding the log display
while minimized. Simulated chat verified these changes.

Chat display and healing addons
Prat changes the chat display. NoHatersBullyElitists can log an original chat event
even when Prat hides it. Clique, HealBot, VuhDo, and Cell have their own healing frames
and click settings. Use distinct global shortcut keys for actions from different addons.

Shared raid icons
DBM, BigWigs, and wMarker can use the same raid icons as Triage. Coordinate marker
assignments with the group so another addon or player does not move an icon you placed.

9. Verification and troubleshooting
Automated Lua checks covered 36 declared version targets with simulated WoW APIs. Tests exercised startup, learned and ranked spells, pet and talent lookup, and party and raid frames. The Retail and Forever regression suite also checked saved clicks, combat deferral, chat routes, and the 40-player raid layout. Live casting and raid performance remain in beta testing.

1. Test the layout and cast gestures solo
In Spell icons, switch between zero and ten and turn off just one tile. With an assigned
heal ready, right-click Self and try its tile. In Custom spells, save a plain and a modified
click. Find both under Saved clicks, choose one to edit, and preview them in Practice.

2. Check combat controls and assignments
Save a spell change in Assign spells during combat. Confirm the old binding works through
the fight and the new one applies after combat. Try the tabs and minimize button too.

3. Verify the full raid roster
Use Raid or Combined in a pickup raid, a guild raid, or Raid Finder. Triage uses WoW's
raid roster for all three. Compare a player in another subgroup with the game's roster.
When Triage opens in an existing raid or after a UI reload, the first refresh fills names
and health. If WoW defers panel setup or subgroup changes in combat, check again when
combat ends.

4. Compare chat and pings with a premade party
With the same teammate, test automatic chat only, native minimap ping only, and both.
Use the same game settings and note when the movement issue appears. To test a manual
alert, use its separate chat and native ping checkboxes. For a quiet public matchmaking
run, leave automatic alerts, both ping options, and the buff announcement unchecked.

5. Retest chat and movement in Raid Finder
Run /triage version and check movement, health bars, and chat with automatic alerts,
native pings, and buff announcements off. If movement stalls, note the time, frame rate,
and WoW's home and world latency. Change one addon at a time when comparing runs.
If you use NoHatersBullyElitists, compare runs with each addon enabled alone and then together.

6. Check Beacon of Faith on a different teammate
Confirm Beacon of Faith is learned in the Holy talent tree. Target a teammate who does
not already have your Beacon of Light. Try the spell from WoW's action bar and then use
its assigned Triage gesture on that teammate. If multiple spells stall, note WoW's exact
error and world latency.

7. Check spell drops, alert controls, and health labels
Drag a learned spell into Assign spells. Return the icon to its source, then save. On a
Retail Paladin with Intercession, check the assigned click on an eligible fallen teammate.
Hover over a raid teammate's !, move away, and right-click it to open the channel menu.
In Appearance, apply each health switch separately, then try both on and both off.

8. Check a talent and its replacement
On a Holy Paladin, choose Holy Prism in the class tree and apply it. Find it in Browse spells
and talents and assign it. Switch hero talents or replace the choice with Divine Toll. Check
the availability status and review the resulting click in Practice. Try a learned tank or
damage spell on another character to check how WoW targets it.
