TriageAegisBeacon.toc belongs directly inside the TriageAegisBeacon folder.
For an update, overwrite the existing addon files. Character settings live in WTF.

Enable the addon on the character screen. In game, run /triage version to check
for 0.1.0 / Build 22 and /triage show to open the panel. In a raid, Party shows
your subgroup, and Raid or Combined shows all subgroups.

Open All heals or /triage heals for the current healing loadout and keys.
Select Show all clicks to look up any assignment by name, ID, or gesture.
The healing panel has matching Left, Middle, Right, and modifier tiles in
Party, Raid, and Combined. Target (/target) shows a selection click, and
hidden or disabled tiles close up automatically. Cast tiles show names and IDs.
Use Emergency or Alt + right-click on an ally's bar for your emergency spell.
Alt + E is the first keyboard choice when available. In combat, use a friendly
mouseover or selected target. Out of combat, Auto target can choose the most
injured eligible group member in confirmed range after checking for a friendly
mouseover. The icon shows the cooldown when the game provides it.
Auto clicks, Auto keys, and Auto target can be turned off in Healing loadout.
Quiet Group in the same place pauses automatic group notices and pings.

Appearance controls shield, heal absorb, and incoming heal strips, plus an
optional Private Aura slot on supported clients. For Classic and WoW Forever,
Appearance also controls personal mana and group threat cues. Where the five-second
mana rule applies, the strip can show an approximate regeneration window.
Practice > Sample cues lets you preview the bar displays and test your clicks.

Type /target into any health-bar spell field to select the clicked unit.
Type None to disable the click. Supported friendly casts on health bars
select their recipient by default. Switch it off in Mouse buttons.

Drop a learned spell from WoW's spellbook or action bar onto a field or Pick.
Return the icon to its source or cancel the drag, then save. Choose where
automatic and manual chat go in Alert settings > Channels. Pick Whisper,
Raid, Say, Battleground, or another destination, then choose Save alerts.
Auto alerts starts off. Native pings and timing are in Behavior.
Each raid teammate has an ! for manual alerts. Appearance has separate
switches for current health and percentages.
Health-bar tooltips show up to three plain clicks. View all assignments
in Settings > Custom spells > Saved clicks.

Use Pick > Browse spells and talents in any spell field. Look up every role
on your current class and specialization. Select an Assignable ability and
save. Passive, unlearned, and replaced entries show their status.
Use Settings > Custom spells > Saved clicks to review your assignments.

See the bundled User-Manual.txt for spell setup, healing shortcuts, alert
controls, and troubleshooting. The PDF edition is a separate download.
